Inkward
Bound

An interactive generative installation working with ink to examine the spatialisation of consciousness.

一件互动生成装置,以墨为材料,探讨意识的空间化。

Ink · LoRA · TouchDesigner · Latent space 墨 · LoRA · TouchDesigner · 潜空间 Scroll ↓ 向下 ↓

As the temporal becoming of consciousness is taken over by AI and flattened into retrievable coordinates, the visitor moves and waits within that space by hand, and the ink follows — diffused, disturbed, condensed.

In the seconds when the system does not obey at once, time regains its thickness, bound inward.

当意识在时间中的生成被 AI 接管、被摊平为可检索的坐标,观众用手在这片空间里 移动、等待,墨随之扩散、扰动、聚拢。

在系统不立即服从的那几秒里,时间重新有了厚度,朝向内里。

01 — The work

01 — 作品

02 — From temporal being to spatial being

02 — 从时间存在性变成空间存在性

Consciousness comes into being in time. A model holds it as coordinates.

Duration, in Bergson's sense, is a multiplicity that cannot be counted: its moments interpenetrate and have no edges. Listening to a melody, the note sounding now is the note it is because the notes before it are still at work inside it.

Things can be counted only because they lie outside one another. Latent space is that condition in its textbook form — vectors occupy coordinates, distances can be measured, values interpolated. To place a continuous process on an axis from 0 to 1 is to give positions to something that has none.

Bergson could argue for a return to duration because the habit he confronted was internal to consciousness, and could be suspended by reflection. The spatialisation at issue here is carried out by technical systems, outside consciousness, and does not change when one's attitude changes. That is why this work does not adjudicate: not as an openness of posture, but because the place from which one might adjudicate no longer exists.

意识在时间中生成,模型把它存成坐标。

柏格森意义上的绵延是数不出来的多:各时刻相互渗透,没有边界。听一段旋律, 此刻这个音之所以是它,是因为前面的音仍在它里面起作用。

能数,是因为彼此外在。潜空间正是这一条件的标准形态——向量各占坐标、 可算距离、可插值。把一个连续的过程放上 0 到 1 的坐标轴,就是给本没有位置的 东西分配位置。

柏格森之所以能主张回到绵延,是因为他面对的是意识内部的习惯,可以反省、 可以悬置。而这里的空间化由技术系统在意识之外执行,不因态度改变而改变。 这正是作品不作裁定的原因:不是姿态上的开放,而是可供裁定的那个位置 已经不存在了。

03 — Why ink

03 — 为何是墨

Ink is not a metaphor for consciousness.

Ink in water interpenetrates, has no edges and cannot be counted. Ink on paper has dried, holds a shape, occupies a position. The material carries both states of the argument — and it is also the earliest technology of spatialisation, the first version of storing anything at all.

It shares an origin with the model, too. Diffusion models move from noise towards form, and that mathematics came out of the study of physical diffusion in the first place — not a coincidence of naming.

墨不是意识的比喻。

水中的墨相互渗透,没有边界,数不出来;纸上的墨干了,有形状,占据位置。 这一材料同时承载了论证的两端——而它本身又是最早的空间化技术, 是"存下来"这件事的第一个版本。

它与模型也同源。扩散模型从噪声走向形态,而这套数学本就源自对物理扩散 过程的研究,不是命名上的巧合。

Three sessions at home across June 2026 produced 54 clips, of which 26 were selected. Four distinct phases were screenshotted from each, retouched to remove air bubbles and tank reflections, and cropped to a monochrome square: 101 training images in four categories — pure diffusion, layered ink, disturbed ink, gathering ink.

2026 年 6 月在家中的三场拍摄共录制 54 段视频,从中精选 26 段。 每段截取四个清晰且有区别的阶段,在 Photoshop 中修去气泡与水缸反光, 裁切为单色方图——最终得到四个类别、共 101 张训练图:自然扩散、 层叠、扰动、聚拢。

04 — How the ink is flattened

04 — 墨如何被摊平

The pipeline performs what the work describes.

管线执行了作品所描述的事。

1Filming → framesThe flow is cut into single frames
2Grouping and captioningStates are named as categories
3LoRA trainingInk becomes a distribution of features
4Latent-space mappingEach state acquires a coordinate
5Multi-seed generation + interpolationRetrievable; continuity stitched back on
操作墨发生了什么
1拍摄 → 切帧流动被切成一格一格
2分组与标注状态被命名为类别
3LoRA 训练墨成为特征分布
4潜空间映射每个状态有了坐标
5多 seed 生成 + 插帧可检索;连续性被缝回

Every step makes the ink more retrievable. The last step attempts to manufacture continuity again — which is precisely what the work is asking about.

Pre-baking is not a compromise; it is the argument. Everything the visitor sees already exists inside a retrievable structure, and nothing is being generated in front of them. If the image really were coming into being in time, the work would contradict itself.

每一步都让墨更可被检索。最后一步试图把连续性重新造出来—— 这正是作品在问的事。

预烘焙不是妥协,而是论点。观众看到的一切都已存在于一个可检索的结构里, 没有任何东西在观众面前"正在生成"。如果画面真的在时间中生成, 作品反而自相矛盾。

05 — The interaction

05 — 交互

Waiting is written into the formula, not asked of the visitor.

Touch produces a single value, c, running from 0 to 1 — from ink dispersed to ink condensed. It is not a readout of position but an integral of the gesture over time: duration of contact carries seven tenths of the weight, steadiness three tenths, and agitation subtracts. Eighteen seconds of holding still are needed to reach the far end. There is no way to simply set it.

Under pressure the value approaches its target smoothly without ever arriving; on release it recedes at a constant rate. What appears depends on the input that came before, not on the command of the moment.

That lag is the material realisation of the Husserlian layer — the slow onset is protention, the slow recession is retention, and the present acquires thickness instead of being a point. The fall is set far slower than the rise: gathering takes more time than dispersing. It is the only part of the work that is genuinely temporal.

等待被写进了公式本身,而不是被要求于观众。

触摸产生一个值 c,从 0 到 1——从墨的弥散到墨的凝结。它不是位置的 读数,而是手势在时间中的积分:按压时长占七成权重,稳定度占三成,躁动则是 减分项。要抵达另一端,需要按住不动整整十八秒。没有办法直接把它设定成某个值。

按压时,这个值向目标平滑逼近却永不到达;松开后,它以恒定速率缓缓退回。 当前画面取决于此前的输入,而非此刻的指令。

那段迟滞是胡塞尔那一层的物质实现——慢启动是前摄,缓慢退回是持存, 当下由此有了厚度,而不再是一个点。回落被设定得远慢于上升: 聚拢比消散更需要时间。它是整件作品里唯一真正具有时间性的部分。

Two screens, neither annotating the other

两块屏幕,谁也不是谁的注解

The projection carries the ink changing state, seen frontally: you are inside it. A second screen carries machine-vision readouts, coordinates and parameters, seen from above: you are over it. One is qualitative multiplicity, the other quantitative. They are present at once and equal in standing — if one had to be read before the other, the second would become an instruction manual, and would imply that the data side is the more real one. Every shift of the eyes performs an act of spatialisation in person.

投影承载墨的状态变化,正面观看:你在里面。副屏承载机器视觉的读数、坐标与 参数,俯视观看:你在上方。一边是质的多样性,另一边是量的多样性。两者同时 在场、地位平等——若必须先读其一再读其二,副屏就成了说明书,并暗示数据 那一边更真实。观众每一次移开视线,都在亲身做一次空间化。

06 — Process

06 — 过程

Seven retrainings, each triggered by a specific failure.

七次重训,每一次都由一个具体的失败推动。

Container features leaking into the trigger word. A flat phase axis. States collapsing at certain seeds. A top-down view that could not be summoned without also summoning literal basins. Captions so generic the model could not tell one frame from another. The full reasoning for every round — including the rounds that did not work — is kept in the process log.

容器特征泄漏进触发词。阶段轴拉不开差异。某些 seed 下状态塌陷。 俯视视角怎么写都会连带召唤出真实的水盆。caption 笼统到模型分不出帧与帧的 区别。每一轮完整的推理——包括那些没有成功的轮次——都记在过程日志里。
