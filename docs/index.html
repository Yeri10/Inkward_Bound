Inkward Bound

An interactive generative installation that uses ink as its main medium to explore the spatialisation of consciousness.

一件互动生成装置,以墨为材料,探讨意识的空间化

As AI takes over the temporal generation of consciousness and flattens it into retrievable coordinates, the visitor moves and stays within this space through the hand, while the ink shifts between diffused, disturbed, and condensed states.

Through this continuous exploration, time regains its thickness, bound inward.

当意识在时间中的生成被 AI 接管,并被摊平为可检索的坐标时,观众用手在这一 空间中移动、停留,墨随之扩散、扰动、凝聚。

在这一持续探索的过程中,时间重新获得了厚度,并向内收拢。

00Research question

00研究问题

When AI compresses consciousness that unfolds over time into retrievable coordinates, how can the body re-enter and experience it?

当 AI 把在时间中展开的意识压缩为可检索的坐标,身体如何重新进入并经验它?

01The work

01作品

02From temporal becoming to spatial being

02从时间性的生成,到空间化的存在

Consciousness unfolds over time, while the model compresses it into spatial coordinates

In Bergson's idea of duration, time is not made up of separate moments. Each moment carries traces of what came before, like a melody: the present note only makes sense because the previous notes are still active within it.

Spatialisation happens when a continuous process is divided, located and compared. Latent space is one such structure: continuous changes that originally have no fixed position are transformed into coordinates that can be located, compared and retrieved.

Datafication further turns continuous flows into elements that can be stored, reorganised and retrieved. What makes AI distinctive is not only its ability to preserve the past, but also to predict what comes next. Recommendation, completion and prediction bring the "next moment" forward, making the present increasingly thin.

This transformation is neither simply a loss nor a form of liberation. It creates new possibilities while also reducing temporal thickness. Within the model, datafied experience enters latent space and becomes a structure that can be located and retrieved.

Yet not all forms of knowing can be fully spatialised. Some knowledge only exists while bodily action is taking place. This is why the work must be experienced through the body, rather than only described to it.

意识随时间生成,而模型将其压缩为空间坐标

在柏格森的"绵延"中,时间不是由彼此分离的瞬间组成的。每一刻都包含此前留下的 痕迹,就像一段旋律:此刻的音之所以成立,是因为前面的音仍在其中持续作用。

空间化发生在连续过程被分解、定位和比较的时候。潜空间正是一种这样的结构: 原本没有固定位置的连续变化,被转化为可以被定位、比较和检索的坐标。

数据化进一步将连续的流转化为可存储、重组和检索的元素。AI 的特殊之处不仅在于 保存过去,更在于预测下一步:推荐、补全与预测让"下一刻"提前出现, 也使当下逐渐变薄。

这种转变既不是单纯的损失,也不是解放。它创造新的可能,同时削弱时间的厚度。 在模型内部,被数据化的经验进入潜空间,成为可定位和检索的结构。

但并非所有"知"都能被完全空间化。有些知识只在身体行动发生的过程中成立。 这也是为什么作品必须通过身体被经验,而不能只被描述。

03Why ink

03为何是墨

Ink is not a metaphor for consciousness

In water, ink spreads into itself without clear boundaries. Once it settles on paper, however, it forms shapes and occupies a fixed position. Ink therefore holds both fluidity and fixity, becoming a material way to explore how a temporal process can be transformed into spatial form.

It also connects to diffusion models. These models generate images by gradually moving from noise towards form, drawing conceptually from processes of physical diffusion. The work brings the diffusion of real ink and computational diffusion into the same system, creating a connection between material process and generative models.

墨不是意识的比喻

水中的墨相互渗透、没有边界;落在纸上后,它却形成形状并占据位置。墨因此 同时呈现了流动与固定,也成为作品中"时间如何被转化为空间"的一种材料形式。

它也与扩散模型形成联系:模型从噪声逐渐生成图像,而这一过程借用了物理扩散 的概念。作品将真实墨水的扩散与计算中的 diffusion 并置,让材料过程与生成 模型发生对应。

04How the ink is spatialised

04墨如何被空间化

The technical process transforms continuous ink movement into a latent space structure that can be classified, located, and retrieved

技术流程将连续的墨水变化转化为可分类、定位和检索的潜空间结构

StepProcessDescription
1Filming & frame extractionThe continuous movement of ink in water is divided into individual frames
2Classification & labellingDifferent ink states are labelled as diffused / disturbed / condensed
3LoRA trainingThe model learns the visual features of different ink states
4Latent space mappingDifferent states are mapped to continuous positions in latent space through a custom c axis
5Frame generation & interpolationMultiple seeds and RIFE interpolation are used to create smooth transitions between states
步骤过程说明
1拍摄与切帧将墨水在水中的连续变化拆分为独立图像
2分类与标注按照弥散 / 扰动 / 凝聚标记不同的墨水状态
3LoRA 训练让模型学习不同墨水状态的视觉特征
4潜空间映射将不同状态映射到潜空间中的连续位置,并建立自定义 c 轴
5帧生成与插值通过多 seed 生成与 RIFE 插值,在不同状态之间建立连续过渡

Every step makes the ink more retrievable. The last step attempts to manufacture continuity again — which is precisely what the work is asking about.

Pre-baking is not a compromise; it is the argument. Everything the visitor sees already exists inside a retrievable structure, and nothing is being generated in front of them. If the image really were coming into being in time, the work would contradict itself.

每一步都让墨更可被检索。最后一步试图把连续性重新造出来—— 这正是作品在问的事。

预烘焙不是妥协,而是论点。观众看到的一切都已存在于一个可检索的结构里, 没有任何东西在观众面前"正在生成"。如果画面真的在时间中生成, 作品反而自相矛盾。

The c axis

The c axis organises eight predefined visual states from 0 to 1. Interaction moves between these states and interpolates the transitions.

cStateDescription
0.0Pure diffusionInk spreads outward freely
0.2Layered inkLayers and fine strands begin to appear
0.4Disturbed inkThe structure breaks apart and becomes unstable
0.5Dense disturbanceThe image becomes darker and more concentrated
0.6Particle stateForms dissolve into fine particles
0.7Early gatheringDark strands begin to move inward
0.8CondensingInk threads gather towards the centre
1.0CondensedThe form becomes dense and temporarily stable

c = 0.6 is the granular-fog stage — the same one the LoRA was retrained seven times to reach.

c 轴

c 轴由 0 到 1 组织八个预设视觉状态。交互会在这些状态之间移动, 并通过插值形成连续过渡。

c状态说明
0.0纯扩散墨水自由向外扩散
0.2层叠墨迹墨迹开始形成层次与细丝结构
0.4扰动墨迹原有结构被打散,状态开始变得不稳定
0.5密集扰动画面进一步变暗、变得更加集中
0.6颗粒状态形态逐渐分解为细小颗粒
0.7初步凝聚深色墨丝开始向内部移动
0.8凝聚中墨丝逐渐向中心聚拢
1.0凝聚形态变得密集,并进入暂时稳定的状态

c = 0.6 是"细颗粒雾"那一档——LoRA 前后重训七次要到达的正是它。

05The interaction

05交互

Movement and stillness become ways of exploring a retrievable space

The audience enters the system through the touchscreen. Sliding and holding are recorded as movement, duration and touch state, and transmitted into the system.

Touch changes the c value in latent space, which controls the projected ink as it moves between diffused, disturbed and condensed states. The system responds in real time: movement keeps the ink in more diffused or disturbed states, while holding increases the c value and moves the image towards condensation.

The visitor is therefore not generating a new image each time. Instead, they are navigating through an existing retrievable visual structure.

The body re-enters the work through this interaction. Some forms of knowing emerge through bodily perception and action and cannot be fully reduced to data or spatial coordinates. Through continuous movement and stillness, the visitor experiences this spatialised computational structure again through time. (Merleau-Ponty; Varela, Thompson & Rosch; Wang Yangming)

移动与停留成为探索可检索空间的方式

观众通过触摸屏进入系统。滑动与停留被记录为移动、持续时间与触摸状态, 并传入系统。

触摸改变潜空间中的 c 值,由它控制投影中的墨在弥散、扰动与凝聚 之间移动。系统实时响应:移动使墨保持在较为弥散或扰动的状态,停留则抬高 c 值,让画面走向凝聚。

因此观众并不是每次都在生成一张新的图像,而是在一个已经存在的、可检索的 视觉结构中穿行。

身体经由这一交互重新进入作品。有些"知"是在身体的知觉与行动中生成的, 无法被完全化约为数据或空间坐标。通过持续的移动与停留,观众在时间中重新 经验这个被空间化的计算结构。 (Merleau-Ponty;Varela、Thompson & Rosch;王阳明)

Interaction logic

交互逻辑

The installation presents three main parts at the same time:

  • Interaction screen — the point where the audience enters the system through touch.
  • Information screen — displays touch coordinates, system parameters, and the original recorded ink dataset on the same screen.
  • Layered projection space — images are projected from both the front and back onto several layers of semi-transparent fabric. The visuals pass through and overlap across these layers, creating a sense of depth rather than a single flat image. This layered space represents the work's latent space: an invisible computational space that is transformed into a physical environment the audience can enter, view, and sense.

These displays are not explanations of each other. Instead, they present different parts of the same system. Bodily movement, data, original footage, and generated visuals exist together in the same space, allowing the audience to experience one interaction from different perspectives.

Through gradual visual change, depth across the layers, and continuous exploration, the interaction is not simply an input-output process. The latent space becomes spatial and perceptible, while duration gains a sense of thickness again. (Bergson; Paglen; Parikka)

装置同时呈现三个主要部分:

  • 交互屏——观众通过触摸进入系统的入口。
  • 信息屏——同时显示触摸坐标、系统参数,以及原始拍摄的墨水数据集。
  • 多层投影空间——影像从正面与背面投射到多层半透明幕布上,并穿透、叠加在不同层之间。墨水因此不再只是一个平面的画面,而形成具有前后关系和空间深度的视觉场域。这里的多层空间也对应作品中的潜空间:一个原本不可见的计算空间,被转化为观众可以进入、观看和感知的物理空间。

这些画面并不是互相解释的说明,而是同一个系统的不同部分。身体动作、数据、 原始影像和生成后的视觉同时存在于空间中,让观众从不同角度感知同一次交互。

通过画面的渐次变化、层与层之间的穿透,以及持续的探索,交互不再只是简单的 "输入—输出"。在这个过程中,潜空间获得了可感知的空间形式,时间也重新获得 了一定的厚度。 (柏格森;Paglen;Parikka)

06Process

06过程

Seven retrainings, each triggered by a specific failure

七次重训,每一次都由一个具体的失败推动

Container features leaking into the trigger word. A flat phase axis. States collapsing at certain seeds. A top-down view that could not be summoned without also summoning literal basins. Captions so generic the model could not tell one frame from another. The full reasoning for every round — including the rounds that did not work — is kept in the process log.

容器特征泄漏进触发词。阶段轴拉不开差异。某些 seed 下状态塌陷。 俯视视角怎么写都会连带召唤出真实的水盆。caption 笼统到模型分不出帧与帧的 区别。每一轮完整的推理——包括那些没有成功的轮次——都记在过程日志里。

Process log →Dated entries: decisions, failures, evidence Dataset log →Capture sessions, selection criteria, processing LoRA training →Pipeline, v1→v7 history, prompt vocabulary
开发流程日志 →带日期的条目:决策、失败与证据 数据集日志 →拍摄场次、筛选标准与处理流程 LoRA 训练 →管线、v1→v7 迭代、prompt 词汇表
